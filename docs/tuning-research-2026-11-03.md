# Motorcycle families and aftermarket dyno research

Reviewed November 3, 2026. All 28 catalog families were checked for two usable aftermarket tuning setups where evidence allowed. Fewer results are retained when the numerical gain, model generation, measurement basis or hardware cannot be verified. This is a bounded research review, not a claim that no other kit exists.

## What changed

- Five added motorcycles: Aprilia Tuareg 660, KTM 790, Kawasaki KLR650, Suzuki DR-Z400, and Honda CB500X. The first four have measured stock curves; the CB500X is clearly source-only.
- Thirty additional upgrade records, including 15 plotted tuned runs. Some are hardware or fuel variants of the same tuner product; they are not presented as independent kits.
- Sixteen additional baseline/stock curves accompany those tunes and the new bikes. The complete library now contains 76 records and 56 usable curves.
- Shared families have one bike-menu entry and separately sourced model weights. Historical 373 cc KTM Duke and Adventure references merge; the 399 cc generation remains separate. Existing numeric shared-link IDs and reference masses are preserved.
- Matched-stock buttons use the same selected mass for both runs. Published gains always refer to their own source baseline, not an unrelated stock result from a different dyno.

## Family and weight rules

KTM 890 appears once, with Adventure, measured Adventure R, newer OEM Adventure R, and Duke R mass references. The Duke dyno runs remain explicitly separate from the Adventure calibration. Similar mass selections cover BMW R 1300 GS/GSA, F 900 GS/GSA, Africa Twin manual/DCT/Adventure Sports, V-Strom 800/800DE, DR-Z4 S/SM, DR-Z400 S/SM, and KLR650 standard/ABS. The original CRF300L Rally and KTM 390 Adventure/Enduro mass presets remain.

A model weight is a scenario denominator, not proof that every variation shares the same power curve. Years, factory calibration, intake, exhaust and emission generation can change power. Selecting another mass does not relabel a source dyno as a test of that body model. OEM curb references and actual measured test weights remain distinguished. Sources for every additional mass are in [catalog-families.json](catalog-families.json); original test weights and original source-point arrays are retained.

## Sampling and exclusions

- New plotted samples follow actual source power traces at bounded RPM positions; they are not scaled from peak claims. [dyno-sampling.json](dyno-sampling.json) records the source asset, sampling canvas, axis calibration, color, and selected pixel for each reading. Pixels were selected near manually identified power traces. Overlapping F 900 GS traces sometimes share a visible line; those points are approximate common-trace readings.
- Power is rounded to 0.1 mechanical hp before deriving torque. RS2E PS and Dimsport CV use `1 PS/CV = 0.9863200706 hp`. The graph then uses the app's existing torque reconciliation/interpolation. Image resolution, chart thickness, sparse samples and correction standards limit accuracy; publisher peak readings are kept separately.
- MotoJP Ténéré uses a speed axis. No guessed speed-to-RPM conversion is used. Low-resolution overlapping 2WDW traces, photographed KLX/Pinit charts, and unknown wheel/crank basis on Tuareg tuner results remain source-only.
- The Carmo CBR650R gallery identifies its lower-power red run as after-flash while another run has the higher maximum. That does not unambiguously demonstrate a gain, so it is excluded. TST's gallery includes a file named for GSX-8S; only its explicit DR-Z4 dyno-gain text is retained.
